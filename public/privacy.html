Privacy Policy

Follow Me: Walk with Jesus, by Third Heaven Studios

Effective October 18, 2026

Follow Me is a Bible game for children and families. We built it so that it collects nothing. This page explains exactly what that means.

Information we collect

None. The game does not collect, store on our servers, share or sell any personal information from anyone, including children. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no in-app purchases, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party advertising or analytics services.

What stays on your device

To let a player continue where they left off, the game saves the following, only on the device it is played on:

  • each player's progress
  • the first name or nickname typed for each player
  • the chosen level
  • memory verses
  • achievements
  • settings

This information is never sent to us or anyone else. The Android app also leaves it out of cloud backups. Deleting a player in the game, or uninstalling the app, removes it.

Permissions

The Android app asks for no special permissions. It does not use the internet, camera, microphone, location, contacts or photos. Spoken lines use the voice engine that is built into the device. That engine reads the text on the device, and the game sends nothing to us.

Children

The game is designed to be safe for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children, as described above, in line with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). The parent area is behind a "press and hold" grown-up check. It contains no links that leave the game.

Website version

The game can also be played in a web browser at followmegame.netlify.app. The website is hosted by Netlify, which may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, to run and protect the service. The game itself sends no information while you play, and it saves progress only in your browser.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
